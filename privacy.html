Privacy Policy

DocFinder for Android (com.najeebkhan.docfinder) and iOS · Developer: Najeeb Khan · Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: DocFinder reads, sorts and searches your documents entirely on your device. Your photos, files and the text found in them are never uploaded — there is no DocFinder server and no account. On Android the app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect limited device and usage data as described below.

1. Data the app handles

DocFinder does not: require an account or sign-in; send your documents or their contents to the developer or anyone else; sell your data; or access your contacts, microphone, camera, precise location or health data.

2. Permissions

  • Photos (Android: read images or selected photos; iOS: Photo Library): used only to scan your photos for documents. On Android 14+ and on iOS you can allow access to selected photos only.
  • All files access (Android 11 and later): used only to find and read PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files across your phone and SD card during a scan. DocFinder uses it read-only: it never modifies, moves or deletes files. You can decline it and still scan your photos. On Android 10 and earlier, storage read permission is used for the same purpose.
  • Keep screen awake: the screen stays on while a scan is running so it is not interrupted.
  • Internet: used for ads (Android) and Google Play update checks. Scanning, sorting and search work offline.

3. Advertising (Android)

The Android app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. Ads never receive your documents, photos, files or the text found in them.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, DocFinder asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The iOS app does not currently show ads. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

4. Third-party components and sharing

  • Google ML Kit performs text recognition on your device. Your photos, files and recognised text are processed locally and are not sent to Google by the app. ML Kit may send limited diagnostic and usage data to Google as described in the ML Kit terms.
  • Google Play in-app updates (Android) checks whether a newer version of DocFinder is available.
  • Google AdMob (Android) — see Advertising.
  • Sharing you choose: when you share a document from DocFinder, it is sent only to the app or person you pick in your phone’s share sheet.

Apart from the above, DocFinder does not share data with any third party.

5. Retention and deletion

Scan results are kept on your device until you remove them. You can remove a single document with Remove from list (your original file is not deleted), or delete all of DocFinder’s data by clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings or uninstalling the app. You can withdraw photo or file access at any time in your phone’s settings.

6. Security

Your documents and scan results are stored only in DocFinder’s private storage on your device, protected by your phone’s own security such as screen lock and device encryption. They are not transmitted over the network.

7. Your choices and rights

Because the developer does not receive or store your documents or scan results, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct or delete — you control it directly on your device as described above. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can use your device’s ad settings and Google’s own privacy controls.

8. Children

DocFinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue on this website’s GitHub repository.
