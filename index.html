Features

Your paperwork, finally in order

Photos of IDs, screenshots of tickets, PDFs from the bank — scattered across your phone. DocFinder pulls them all into one organised place.

Automatic scanning

One tap scans every photo and screenshot, plus every PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint file on your phone. No picking files one by one.

Reads text in photos

Built-in text recognition reads photos and scanned PDF pages, so even a picture of a receipt becomes searchable.

30 smart categories

Passports, bank statements, invoices, medical reports, contracts and more — sorted automatically into 7 easy groups.

Search by any number

Type an ID number however you like — with spaces, dashes or just part of it — and DocFinder still finds the right document.

Key details, one tap to copy

ID numbers, names, dates, IBANs, totals and masked card numbers are pulled out for you. IBANs and card numbers are checked for validity.

Picks up where it left off

Stop a scan any time and it resumes later. New files are added, edited files are re-read, and deleted files drop off the list.

How it works

Organised in three steps

No setup, no sign-up. Install the app and let it do the sorting.

Scan your phone

Tap Scan everything. DocFinder goes through your photos and files, showing live progress and time left. You can keep using your phone.

It sorts for you

Each document is read and filed into the right category — from National ID to Lab Report — with its important details pulled out.

Find anything, fast

Browse by category or search by a number, a name, a date or any word. Open, share, rename or move a document in a tap.

Smart search

Type it any way. Find it every time.

ID numbers are written in a dozen different ways. DocFinder ignores the formatting, so the whole number, the number without dashes, or just a part of it all lead to the same document — and it shows you exactly why it matched.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. DocFinder reads, sorts and searches your documents entirely on your phone. There is no DocFinder server and no account, so your photos, files and the text found in them stay on your device.

Is DocFinder free?

Yes, DocFinder is free to download and use. On Android it shows a small banner ad, which helps support development.

Which files can it find?

Photos and screenshots (JPG, PNG and other image formats), PDFs, and Word, Excel and PowerPoint files. Scanned PDF pages without a text layer are read with text recognition too.

Why does it ask for “All files access” on Android?

On Android 11 and later, this permission is the only way an app can find PDF and Office files across your whole phone and SD card. DocFinder uses it only to read those documents during a scan — it never changes, moves or deletes your files. You can skip it and still scan your photos.

Will it change or delete my files?

Never. Your original files stay exactly where they are. “Remove from list” only removes a document from DocFinder’s list, not from your phone.

A document is in the wrong category. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open the document and move it to another category, or rename it. DocFinder remembers your choice and will not override it later.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Scanning, sorting and search all work offline.

When is the iPhone version coming?

The iOS version is in development. The App Store link will be added to this page as soon as it is live.
